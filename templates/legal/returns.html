Returns & Exchanges

We offer a 30-day return policy from the date of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Return Policy

You may return most new, unopened items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. We'll also pay the return shipping costs if the return is a result of our error.

Eligible for Return:

  • Unused items in original packaging
  • Items with all tags and labels attached
  • Items in resalable condition
  • Proof of purchase (order number)

Non-Returnable Items:

  • Used or worn footwear
  • Items without original packaging or tags
  • Customized or personalized items
  • Items marked as final sale

Refund Timeline

Once we receive your return, we will inspect it and process your refund within 5-7 business days. The refund will be credited to your original payment method.
