# Newsletter System - Simplified Guide

## How It Works Now

The newsletter system has been **simplified** - admins only need to fill in **one content field** instead of two!

### What You Need to Fill In

When creating a newsletter, you only need to provide:

1. **Subject** - The email subject line
2. **Content** - Your newsletter content

That's it! No more confusing "plain text content" field.

---

## Writing Newsletter Content

You can write your content in **simple HTML** or **plain text**. The system will automatically handle both HTML and plain text versions.

### Example 1: Simple Text

```
Hello!

Check out our latest products this week:
- New smartphones
- Laptops on sale
- Free shipping on all orders

Visit our store: http://localhost:8000/products/

Thanks!
```

### Example 2: With Simple HTML

```html
<h1>Weekly Newsletter 🎉</h1>

<p>Hello valued customer!</p>

<p>Check out our <strong>latest products</strong> this week:</p>

<ul>
  <li>New smartphones</li>
  <li>Laptops on sale - <strong>20% off!</strong></li>
  <li>Free shipping on all orders</li>
</ul>

<p style="text-align: center;">
  <a href="http://localhost:8000/products/" class="button">
    Shop Now
  </a>
</p>

<p>Thanks for being a loyal customer!</p>
```

### Supported HTML Tags

You can use these simple HTML tags:
- `<h1>`, `<h2>`, `<h3>` - Headings
- `<p>` - Paragraphs
- `<strong>` or `<b>` - Bold text
- `<em>` or `<i>` - Italic text
- `<ul>` and `<li>` - Bullet lists
- `<a href="...">` - Links
- `<br>` - Line breaks

---

## What Happens Automatically

When you send the newsletter:

1. **HTML Version** - Your content is wrapped in the professional Fleeto email template with logo
2. **Plain Text Version** - Automatically generated by removing HTML tags
3. **Unsubscribe Link** - Automatically added at the bottom
4. **Fleeto Branding** - Logo, colors, and signature added automatically

---

## Quick Start

1. Go to `/admin` → Notifications → Newsletter Campaigns
2. Click "Add Newsletter Campaign"
3. Fill in:
   - **Subject**: "Weekly Deals at Fleeto!"
   - **Content**: Write your message (with or without HTML)
4. Click "Save"
5. Click "Send Newsletter" button
6. Confirm and send!

---

## No More Confusion!

**Before:** Had to fill "Content" AND "Plain text content" (confusing!)  
**Now:** Just fill "Content" once (simple!)

The system automatically creates the plain text version for email clients that don't support HTML.
